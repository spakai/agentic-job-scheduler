Focused test failures and correction:
- Initial run: two ProcessingWindowTest errors because a test requested a batch
  larger than its configured max-fetch count/bytes.
- Next run: two incorrect expectations assumed resumption before both adjusted
  watermarks were below half and assumed a rejected reservation changed counters.
- Corrected fixtures to use valid fetch sizes and asserted the actual hysteresis
  and refusal semantics; the final focused and full test runs passed.

What the tests establish:
- Pause at either count or byte high watermark.
- Resume only below half of both adjusted watermarks.
- One configured outstanding fetch may cross a high watermark without exceeding
  the configured hard processing-window bound.
- Invalid, foreign, or double-released reservations are rejected.
- A reservation remains counted after a failed offset commit and is released only
  after a later commit succeeds.
- The Vert.x Kafka consumer pauses its assigned partition at the count
  watermark, continues polling without receiving records while paused, then
  resumes record delivery only after offset 4 is committed and reservations
  are released.
